Choose a Letter

Your wild tile (✱) can stand for any letter.

How to Play

  • 🖱️Click a tile in your rack to select it, then click a board cell to place it.
  • ↩️Click a placed tile on the board to return it to your rack.
  • 📝Form words reading left→right or top→bottom. Words must be 2+ letters.
  • ⭐The center ★ square must be covered before you can submit.
  • ✖️DL/TL double/triple a letter score. DW/TW double/triple the whole word.
  • 🃏Wild tiles (✱) can be any letter — choose when you place them.
  • 🗑️Clear returns all tiles to your rack. 🔀 shuffles the rack display.
